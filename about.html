About Saddlebag Opal

An independent, brand-neutral guide to saddle bags, crossbody styling and leather care.

Our story

Why the saddle bag?

Few bag shapes are as instantly recognisable as the curved saddle bag. It mixes practical riding history with playful modern design, and it suits almost every wardrobe.

Saddlebag Opal explains how these bags are made, how to wear them comfortably and how to keep leather looking its best, without promoting any label.
