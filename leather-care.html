The leather care guide

Simple routines to clean, condition, store and protect leather bags so they last for years.

The basics

Little and often

Leather is a natural material that responds to its environment. A few minutes of regular care does more than an occasional deep clean.

  • Dust with a soft cloth after use.
  • Keep away from heat, damp and direct sun.
  • Condition only when the leather looks dry.
  • Store stuffed, in a breathable dust bag.

Storage

Storing bags between seasons

  1. Empty all pockets and wipe the lining.
  2. Fill the bag with acid-free tissue to keep its curve.
  3. Detach long straps and store them flat or loosely coiled.
  4. Place in a cotton dust bag, not plastic.
  5. Keep upright on a shelf with space around it.
